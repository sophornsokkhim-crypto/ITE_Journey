What I learned

  • What is Technology? How to solve the problem by using that technology?
  • General knowledge
  • Designing
  • How to transfer the theory of Math and Physics to solve the real problem
  • Programming languages
  • How to make Website and System

One of the most important lessons I learned as an ITE student is to learn technology, not just follow what the lecturer teaches. Lecturers cannot teach us everything, so we should take what we learn in class as a starting point, research more about each topic, and apply our knowledge to real-world projects and problems.

Advices for juniors

  • Search for more information
  • Practice what you learn
  • build small project
  • Learn from mistakes
  • Don't be afraid of difficult subjects
  • Improve your english
  • Learn how to use documentations
  • Ask the questions when you stuck
  • Manage your time
  • Focus on understanding, not memorizing
