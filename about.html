About the major

What is ITE?

Explore the field of Information Technology Engineering and discover what makes it an important part of modern technology.

Information Technology Engineering (ITE) is a field that combines programming, computer systems, networking, databases, software development, and other technologies.

It applies scientific, mathematical, and practical design principles to build, scale, and secure digital infrastructure. Rather than just using technology, ITE focuses on engineering brand-new systems from scratch — making sure massive volumes of data can flow reliably and safely between hardware, software, and millions of users at the same time.

In short: ITE isn't just about knowing how to use a computer — it's about understanding and building the systems that make computers (and the internet) work in the first place.

The areas ITE brings together

💻

Programming & software development

Writing the logic that makes applications actually do something.

🗄️

Databases

Storing and organizing data so it can be found and used reliably.

🌐

Networking & data communications

Getting information from one machine to another, quickly and safely.

🖥️

Computer systems

Understanding the hardware and infrastructure software runs on.
